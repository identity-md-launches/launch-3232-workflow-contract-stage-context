# Not Firm: notSTOCK $NOTSTOCK

Binding specification of one Robinhood Chain (chain id 4663) company launch: its contracts and the NOTSTOCK token. Build exactly what is written here.

## 0. Binding rules

- ALL NUMBERS, ADDRESSES AND RULES IN THIS FILE ARE FIXED: if one cannot be met, fail the job, never change it.
- Adapt only what the launch rules or a reproduced finding require, each change in ADAPTATION.md. Never change rates, recipients, limits or the token, never add an owner power, pause, upgrade or minting.

- LpDesk never deposits a position NFT into any staking, gauge or farm contract; positions earn only pool fees. (fail, don't change)

## 2a. LpDesk governance (LpGovernor, an LpDesk module)

- Staking: any NOTSTOCK holder may stake(amount) into LpGovernor at any time; staked NOTSTOCK stays in LpGovernor and counts as that holder's voting weight. unstake(amount) is always allowed and returns NOTSTOCK immediately; a proposal's voting weight is fixed at the staked balance of each holder at the end of the block before the one in which the proposal was created (checkpoint), so a later unstake cannot be used to vote twice or to undo a cast vote; the quorum base is the total staked at that same checkpoint. LpGovernor moves NOTSTOCK only back to its staker. (fail, don't change)
- Proposal: a holder with a staked balance of at least 0.1% of all currently staked NOTSTOCK, or the operator with any staked balance, zero included, may create one proposal of kind OpenPosition(asset, venue, fee tier, initial range, price, amount), AddAsset(asset), AddVenue(positionManager, kind), AddBridge(adapter, dstChainId, dstLpDesk) or BridgeOut(bridge id, amount); an OpenPosition or BridgeOut amount above LpDesk's free balance at creation or at execute() reverts. At most one open proposal of each kind at a time from non-operator authors, and at most one of each kind from the operator; the operator's proposal is not blocked by another author's. A proposal is open until it is executed, fails, is vetoed or expires (Execute below). (fail, don't change)
- Bridge: an executed AddBridge stores a permanent bridge record in LpGovernor: adapter (the LayerZero OFT v2 adapter of NOTSTOCK, deployed later by a separate order; AddBridge reverts at creation and at execute() unless adapter.token() is the registered NOTSTOCK), dstChainId (the LayerZero endpoint id of the destination chain) and dstLpDesk (this launch's LpDesk on that chain). An executed BridgeOut makes LpDesk approve exactly amount of free NOTSTOCK to that record's adapter, call its send() with dstEid = dstChainId, to = dstLpDesk, amountLD = minAmountLD = amount, refund address = the execute() caller, the native messaging fee paid by that caller as execute()'s msg.value, then reset the approval to 0. No recipient other than the record's dstLpDesk is possible; an unregistered bridge id, a missing vote or an amount above free NOTSTOCK reverts. Only NOTSTOCK is bridged. (fail, don't change)
- Vote: for 3 days from creation, any staker may cast its full checkpointed weight once, for or against; weight cannot be moved between proposals or split. (fail, don't change)
- Pass condition: total weight cast reaches a quorum of 3% of all NOTSTOCK staked at that checkpoint, and for-weight exceeds against-weight. Missing either condition when the 3 days end fails the proposal permanently; a failed proposal may be resubmitted later as a new proposal. (fail, don't change)
- Veto window: a passed proposal enters a 2-day window in which the operator alone may call veto() once, cancelling it permanently. veto() only cancels; it cannot alter the proposal or substitute another action. (fail, don't change)
- Execute: once the 2-day veto window ends with no veto, execute() is callable by any address and performs exactly the proposal's OpenPosition, AddAsset, AddVenue, AddBridge or BridgeOut action, once (AddBridge inside LpGovernor, the others by calling LpDesk); LpDesk accepts these actions only from its own LpGovernor, and PriceGuard accepts AddAsset and AddVenue only from its own LpDesk. An OpenPosition's price is fixed at creation: if at execute() the pool's current price differs from it by more than 0.5%, execute() reverts, and the open may be proposed again as a new proposal. Expiry, for every kind: a passed proposal not executed within 259 200 seconds (3 days) after its veto window ends expires; execute() on an expired proposal reverts. An expired, failed or vetoed proposal does not count as open and does not block a new proposal of its kind; a failed or vetoed one stops blocking as soon as its outcome is set. (fail, don't change)
- Read: LpDesk and its modules expose view functions listing every open position (venue, pair, range, liquidity, pending fees), the venue list (venues(): position manager, kind, factory (for V4 its PoolManager)), the asset list, per token the free balance, top-up basket and buyback basket, the bridge records and BridgeOut totals, the staked supply, the bootstrap end time, and every proposal's state (including Expired), vote weights, window deadlines and expiry time; no other contract or server computes this report. (fail, don't change)
